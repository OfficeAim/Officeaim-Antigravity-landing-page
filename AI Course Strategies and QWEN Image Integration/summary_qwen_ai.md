
## Summary of Qwen.AI Image Generation Capabilities

Qwen.AI, particularly its Qwen-Image model, is a powerful AI image generation tool. Key capabilities include:

*   **Text-to-Image Generation:** Transforms text descriptions into a wide range of visually stunning images.
*   **Image Editing:** Enables advanced operations such as style transfer, object insertion or removal, and detail enhancement.
*   **Complex Text Rendering:** Achieves significant advances in rendering complex text within generated images, including bilingual prompts (Chinese and English).
*   **Open-Source:** It is an open-source image foundation model, making it accessible for various applications.
*   **Versatility:** It can be used for generating aesthetic and photorealistic images, and for tasks like creating logos or visual assets for presentations and marketing.

This tool appears to be highly suitable for generating visual content for the user's AI courses and website, especially given its capabilities in precise image editing and text rendering.
