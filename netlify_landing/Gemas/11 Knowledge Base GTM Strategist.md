1 . THE  NORTH  STAR  M  ETRIC  (NSM  )
A successful GTM strategy hinges on tracking the right metric. Never track vanity metrics
(registered users, logins, pageviews). Track "Core Value Events".
● B a d  NSM  : "Daily Active Logins."
● G  o o d  NSM  : "Weekly Activated Users who completed 3 tasks and shared a workspace."
Productivity and SaaS apps die when they measure intent instead of value delivered.
2 . THE  5  GTM   PHASES
● P h a s e  0  (M  a rk e t T h e s is ): Define the beachhead segment. Set up tracking (Mixpanel,
Amplitude). Define the positioning one-liner.
● P h a s e  1  (V a lid a tio n ): 30-50 customer interviews. Test willingness to pay. Track
Time-to-Value (TTV).
● P h a s e  2  (C lo s e d  B e ta ): Invite-only (200-1,000 users). Goal is to prove D7 (Day 7) and
Week 4 retention. Fix friction in onboarding.
● P h a s e  3  (P u b lic  L a u n c h ): Launch on Product Hunt, targeted subreddits, and newsletters.
Shift focus from retention to scalable acquisition (CAC).
● P h a s e  4  (G ro w th  &  M  o n e tiz a tio n ): Activate Product-Led Growth (PLG) loops. Test
freemium limits. Optimize LTV:CAC to a 3:1 ratio.
3 . THE  3  LAUNCH  APPROACHES
1. B ig  B a n g  (P R  +  P a id ): High risk. Best when PMF is already strong and budget is
meaningful. Wastes momentum if retention is poor.
2. N ic h e  B e a c h h e a d : Best for B2B/Productivity. Target one specific community (e.g.,
"Agency Project Managers"). High activation, clear positioning.
3. E x c lu s iv e  C lo s e d  B e ta : Invite-only. Best for products needing heavy iteration. Builds
FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) and strong case studies.
4 . CRIT ICAL  STARTUP  KPIs  T O  TRACK
● TTV  (T im  e  T o  V a lu e ): Minutes/hours until the user experiences the product's core
promise.
● A c tiv a tio n  R a te : % of signups who hit the core value event in 24-72 hours.
● D 7  R e te n tio n : % of users who return on Day 7. (Consumer/SaaS: 25% is okay, 40%+ is
great).
● LTV :CAC : Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost ratio. Target is 3:1 or higher.
● P a y b a c k  P e rio d : How many months it takes to recover CAC. Aim for < 6-12 months.
