Security & Privacy at OfficeAIM

Enterprise-grade protection for your AI-powered workflows.

Protecting Your Business Intelligence

At OfficeAIM, protecting your proprietary data, business ideas, and workflows is our top priority. We provide a highly available and secure environment so you can build, automate, and scale with confidence. This document outlines our security-by-design architecture.

AI & LLM Data Privacy (Zero-Training Policy)

The most critical aspect of enterprise AI is data privacy. OfficeAIM connects to advanced Large Language Models (LLMs) via secure, enterprise-grade APIs.

  • Your Data is Yours: The prompts, data, and context you provide to our AI Agents are never used to train public foundational models (such as public versions of Gemini or OpenAI).
  • Ephemeral Processing: Data sent to the AI is processed strictly to return your generated response and is not permanently stored by the LLM providers.

Infrastructure & Data Encryption

We partner with industry-leading infrastructure providers to ensure your data is safe at rest and in transit.

  • Database Engine: OfficeAIM utilizes Supabase (powered by PostgreSQL) as our core database engine.
  • Encryption: All customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. Data in transit between your browser, our servers, and our AI nodes is encrypted using HTTPS and TLS 1.2+ protocols.
  • Cloud Hosting: Our architecture is hosted on secure cloud infrastructure, ensuring high availability, redundancy, and automated backups.

Workflow Automation Security

Our automation node processes your business logic with strict isolation.

  • Secure Webhooks: All data routed through our automation nodes (via n8n) relies on authenticated, encrypted webhook endpoints.
  • Credential Management: API keys and external integrations are securely hashed and stored. They are only injected at runtime to execute your specific workflows.

Access Control & Identity

OfficeAIM employs strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

  • Workspace Isolation: Student and Admin workspaces are strictly isolated. No user can access the prompts, history, or generated assets of another user.
  • Authentication: We use secure session tokens and JWT (JSON Web Tokens) to continuously validate your identity during active sessions.

Data Retention & Deletion

You maintain full control over your data.

  • Active Vault: Your generated assets are stored in your personal Vault for as long as your account is active.
  • Right to Erasure: Upon request, or upon account deletion, all personal data and generated assets are permanently wiped from our active databases and automation nodes in compliance with global privacy standards.

Shared Responsibility

While OfficeAIM secures the platform and infrastructure, customers are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials and ensuring the appropriateness of the data they input into the prompt generators. Please avoid inputting highly sensitive personal health information (PHI) or unencrypted cardholder data (CHD) into the AI agents.
