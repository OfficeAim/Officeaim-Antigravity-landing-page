1. THE GOLDEN RULE OF FUNNELS
Every step in a funnel has exactly ONE conversion goal.
● The Ad's job is to get the click (not sell the product).
● The Landing Page's job is to get the email (not explain the whole methodology).
● The Webinar/VSL's job is to shift beliefs and make the pitch.
● The Checkout Page's job is to remove risk and capture payment.
2. CRITICAL WEBINAR / VSL FUNNEL KPIs
● Registration Page Conversion: 20-40% (depending on traffic warmth).
● Show-up Rate (Live): 25-30% is standard.
● 10-Minute Retention: Crucial metric. If they drop off before 10 mins, your hook failed.
● Offer Click Rate: % of attendees who click the CTA when the pitch is made.
● Order Page Conversion: How many who saw the price actually bought.
3. HIGH-CONVERTING LEVERS BY STAGE
● Thank You Page (Post-Optin): Don't waste this page. Use it for a "Micro-commitment"
(e.g., "Add to calendar", "Join the WhatsApp group", or a 1-question survey).
● Reminder Sequence: Use "Just-in-time" reminders (24h, 3h, 1h, 15m) via both Email and
SMS.
● The Pitch Transition: Use a bridge sentence: "If you want help implementing this faster..."
Position the offer as speed, certainty, and support.
● The Checkout Page: Place Risk Reversal (guarantee) right next to the buy button. Ensure
tight alignment between what was pitched and what is on the page (no bait-and-switch).
4. THE POST-FUNNEL FOLLOW-UP
Always split the follow-up based on user behavior:
● Attended but didn't buy: Send objection-handling emails (Time, Money, Fit, Confidence).
Add ethical urgency (bonus expiring).
● Registered but no-show: Send the replay, but include a "Start here" timestamped email
so they don't feel overwhelmed. Offer a shorter "best-of" 15-minute cut.
