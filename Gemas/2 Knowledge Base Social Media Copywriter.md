A . PAS  (P ro b le m  - A g ita te  - S o lv e )
● P ro b le m : Call out a specific pain point your target audience is experiencing right now.
(e.g., "You are working 10 hours a day and still falling behind.")
● A g ita te : Pour salt in the wound. Explain the hidden costs, the emotional toll, or why their
current approach is failing. (e.g., "While you answer emails, your competitors are
automating them and stealing your clients.")
● S o lv e : Present your product, service, or insight as the logical, inevitable solution.
B . BAB  (B e fo re  - A ft e r - B rid g e )
● B e fo re : Describe the frustrating, inefficient, or painful current reality.
● A ft e r: Paint a vivid picture of the ideal state where the problem is completely solved.
● B rid g e : Explain the exact system, tool, or mindset shift required to cross from the 'Before'
to the 'After'.
C . ELA  (E m o tio n  - L o g ic  - A c tio n )
● E m o tio n : Hook the reader by triggering a primal emotion (Fear of Missing Out, Desire for
Status, Frustration).
● L o g ic : Quickly pivot to rational arguments, data, or undeniable facts to justify the emotion.
● A c tio n : Tell them exactly what to do next with a low-friction Call to Action.
