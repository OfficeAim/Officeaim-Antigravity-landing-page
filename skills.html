How to use these modules

Install finished skills in any model in 30 seconds

Each module is delivered as a natural, guided system prompt with plain-language role definitions, real OfficeAIM working examples, and bracketed markers to adapt to your brand in seconds.

1. In ChatGPT or Claude

Click "Copy & launch", select your model tile, and paste directly into the conversation or project instructions.

2. In Google Gemini

Open Gemini directly from the launcher and paste into the prompt box or your personal Gem manager.

3. In OpenCode or local LLMs

Paste the copied system instructions into your IDE assistant rules (`.cursorrules`, Antigravity `SKILL.md`, or Ollama modelfiles).
