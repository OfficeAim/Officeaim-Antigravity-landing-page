Common questions

Can I really start for free?

Yes. No credit card, no trial period. Your free account gives you access to 6 specialist AI agents and Módulos 1 & 2 of the curriculum — forever.

What happens to my sessions if I downgrade?

Your Neural Vault sessions are always saved. If you downgrade, you lose access to Pro/Plus agents, but all your previous work remains accessible.

What is the Idea Cloner and why is it free?

The Idea Cloner is our SaaS Blueprint Architect — it reverse-engineers any successful business into a 90-day launch plan. It's unlocked when you complete Módulo 2, rewarding students who invest in learning.

Why does the Behavioral Psychologist run on a Pro model?

It's a deliberate gift to free users — a Plus-tier intellect deployed freely so you can experience the depth of premium agents before upgrading. It runs on Gemini 3.5 Flash.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Pro and Plus are monthly subscriptions. Cancel anytime and you keep access until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees.

What are the Gemini Gem Agents?

These are 15 specialized AI agents built inside Google Gemini — not OfficeAIM's Hub. No login required. Think of them as a free preview of AI-assisted work, powered directly by Google.
